Architectural Invariant

The Triad of Responsibility

Non-negotiable architectural separation: AI reasons over evidence, Python computes all numbers, Humans authorize all dollars.

🤖

1. AI / LLM Domain

Evidence Synthesis & Parsing

  • ✓ Gathers unstructured signals from maritime AIS, news & SEC filings.
  • ✓ Extracts force majeure, penalty clauses & lead times from supplier contracts.
  • ✓ Synthesizes risk dossiers with zero arithmetic permissions.
  • ✕ ZERO math calculations or financial decisions permitted.
⚙️

2. Deterministic Domain

100% Python Arithmetic

  • ✓ Computes exact Days of Cover (current stock / daily rate).
  • ✓ Computes Shortage Volume down to individual SKU units.
  • ✓ Calculates Financial Exposure ($2,448,105.00 for NanoCore).
  • ✓ Guaranteed zero-hallucination arithmetic compliance.
🛡️

3. Human Authority

Cryptographic HITL Spend Gate

  • ✓ Category Manager approval required for spend ≤ $100k.
  • ✓ VP Supply Chain dual-signature required for spend > $100k.
  • ✓ AWS KMS ECDSA P-256 cryptographic token minting & replay protection.
  • ✓ Zero autonomous purchase orders or PO releases.
